Privacy Policy for Dodon't

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Dodon't ("the game") is an arcade maze game for Android. This policy explains what the game does with your data. In short: the game has no accounts and no server of ours, your scores stay on your device, and the only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service collects in order to show ads.

Data the game keeps on your device

The game stores your personal best result, your sound and vibration settings, and a count of the runs you have finished (used only to decide when an ad may show). This is stored on your device. We do not receive, store or see any of it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game has no user accounts and no leaderboard, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting service of ours.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional ad you may choose to watch to continue a run, and a full-screen ad shown between some runs. To deliver and measure these ads, Google's advertising software inside the game collects data from your device and sends it to Google. This can include your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), information about your device and the game (such as model, operating system version and language), and how you interact with the ads. Google may use this data to show personalised ads, to limit how often you see an ad, and to detect fraud.

We do not receive this data ourselves, other than summary figures in our AdMob account such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice about ad personalisation before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form. Ads are requested only after you have made that choice.

On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Settings > Google > Ads (the exact wording depends on your device).

The game can be played without an internet connection. In that case no ads are shown and no data is sent.

Sharing a score card

If you tap "Share", the game creates a picture of your result and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you tap that button, and the picture goes only where you send it. We do not receive it.

Children

The game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
